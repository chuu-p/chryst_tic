# Cult of Fayth

The Cult of Fayth is a dominant religion rooted in a syncretic blend of ancient Nordic paganism and a deeply compassionate interpretation of Christianity, heavily influenced by Tibetan Buddhist ideals of universal love and selflessness. Their central thesis is radical compassion: that one must not lose love even for their darkest enemies or oppressors.

## Philosophy and Magic

In this world, gods are real, and belief is the protocol that interfaces with their divine power. Followers of the Cult of Fayth tap into the golden winds of Fayth to perform healing magic and weave protective enchantments. Prayers are literal source code—structured algorithms that manipulate the Fayth magic field.

Fayth energy traverses the air much like a hybrid of light and lightning: invisible, traveling at the speed of light, yet seeking paths of resistance through the atmosphere. It emits a subtle warmth. Skilled priests can script it to heat and illuminate homes. With the burning of holy smoke, as practiced by initiates like [[Fyrchtegott]], these golden veins become visible to the naked eye.

## Background

Born from the bitter cold and unrelenting hardships of the deep North. The Cult realized that survival required absolute unity against the monsters and chaos of the early eras.

### Early Life

The religion began as small scatterings of pagan worshippers hiding in ice-caves. They relied on primitive communal magic to survive the eternal winters.

### Turning Point 1

The revelation of the 'Source Code'. [[Frydemann]] inscribed the first iteration of the Bible, transforming erratic, primitive magic into structured, highly computational golden winds of Fayth.

### Turning Point 2

The establishment of the Sunday Pulse, transforming the unstructured congregation into a singular continent-spanning power grid.

## Architecture and Rituals

The holiest text, the Bible, is a master program written by [[Frydemann]]. It contains conditional syntax and arrays of prayer-scripts. For instance, putting a handwritten script inside a church and fueling it with Sunday mass "charges" the aura, creating a localized ward that reduces monster incursions by 80%.

Fayth is generated by communal prayer, living righteously, and believing in the Goddess. It is stored in churches, holy artifacts, and _Frozen Hearts_. When a devout follower dies, their heart can be steeped in icy, resin-like gem enclosures. Holy prayer runes are carved deep into the crystal, causing the dead heart to beat once every Sunday, sustaining the network.

## Appearance and Priests

### Appearance

Priests wear thick, layered vestments suitable for the biting cold of the Nordic realms—often deep greys and muted blues adorned with golden thread. Their hands are wrapped in prayer beads inscribed with holy syntax. The frozen hearts of martyrs are sometimes carried in heavy, frost-bitten reliquaries on the backs of senior clerics.

### Personality

Pious, profoundly empathetic, yet burdened by the sorrow of the world. They operate with solemn diligence, acting as both spiritual guides and arcane engineers of their community's protective network.

# CHARACTER MAP

CHARACTER NAME: Cult of Fayth (As a Faction Entity)

## ORIGIN OF URGE

Born from the bitter cold and unrelenting hardships of the deep North, the Cult realized that survival required absolute unity and a love strong enough to shield against both literal monsters and the encroaching darkness of the human heart.

## CORE URGE

**To shepherd humanity toward a painless eternity through unity and faith.**
In order to survive and get their needs met, they must connect everyone to the holy network and preserve the souls of the departed in Frozen Hearts for eventual salvation.

## STATED BELIEF (LIE)

The Goddess wills our suffering today so that we may earn the paradise of tomorrow.

## THE CORE URGE AFFECTS ALL AREAS OF THEIR LIFE

### GOALS

Constructing an omnipresent Fayth network that connects all living souls and safely stores the dead until the promised resurrection.

### RELATIONSHIPS

Deeply paternalistic and communal, though occasionally overbearing, demanding absolute integration into the Sunday Pulse.

### LIFESTYLE

Ascetic but warm; communities are built around the central church's golden radiance.

### PRESENTATION

Humble, sorrowful, but harboring an undeniable inner light.

### DIALOGUE

"We freeze the heart to thaw the soul. Forgive the blade that strikes you, for the wielder knows not the cold they invite upon themselves."
