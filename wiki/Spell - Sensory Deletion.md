# Spell: Sensory Deletion

## Description

Sensory Deletion is a shadow spell that temporarily deletes an enemy's sight, hearing, and physical sensation, rendering them helpless.

## Mechanics

- **Effect**: Removes sight, hearing, and physical sensation
- **Type**: Shadow spell
- **Duration**: Temporary

## Usage

Used by [[Berlynde]] and shadow mages of the [[Cult of Maxt]] to disable enemies by completely cutting off their connection to the physical world.
