# Spell: Frozen Heart

## Description

Frozen Heart is a passive ability where a second heart frozen in cyan resin stores excess Fayth energy and grants 50% resistance to mind control effects.

## Mechanics

- **Effect**: Stores excess Fayth, grants 50% mind control resistance
- **Type**: Passive
- **Visual**: Cyan resin-encased secondary heart

## Usage

Possessed by [[Chryst]] and advanced practitioners of the [[Cult of Fayth]] who have undergone the freezing ritual to enhance their mental defenses.
