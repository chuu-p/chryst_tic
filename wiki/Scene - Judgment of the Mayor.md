Brsykyr, the jewel of [[Norholdt]], lay under the cold shadow of [[Maxt]] occupation. [[Chryst]] and [[Astryd]] (then known as Axtritt) were brought before the [[Brsykyr occupational Mayor]], whose office smelled of old parchment and the sharp tang of iron. The Mayor, a man whose soul seemed as rigid as his armor, looked upon them with eyes that had long ago forgotten the meaning of mercy.

"Axtritt," the Mayor’s voice was a low rumble, "you have been found wanting. You were saved by a stranger when you should have died with honor. To Maxt, you are a ghost. You are hereby dishonorably discharged." He gestured to Tymo, shackled nearby, and then to Astryd. "Both of you are now property of the city. You will be put up as prizes in the tournament—the champion claims you as slaves, along with a chest of gold. Unless someone buys your freedom."

Astryd stood silent, the weight of her stripped rank and new status heavier than any blade. It was then that Chryst stepped into the dim light.

"My Lord," Chryst began, his voice calm but resonant. "I seek not peace, but the right to practice my faith within these stone walls. Grant me the certification of a priest, and let the [[Cult of Fayth]] be recognized as a sanctuary for those who have lost their way."

The Mayor let out a cold, rasping laugh. "You ask for a sanctuary in a city built on strength? Very well, Priest. I will offer you a wager. The annual tournament begins in three days. In our culture, strength is the only currency of respect. If you can emerge as the champion, I will allow your religion to be practiced, certification shall be yours, any crimes you have committed shall be washed away in the blood of the arena, and you may claim the two slaves as your own—free to do with them as you wish. If you fail... then you shall find the silence you so desire."

Chryst inclined his head. "I accept the wager. Let the goddess be my witness. But I will not fight for gold—I fight for their freedom."
