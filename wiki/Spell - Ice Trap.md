# Spell: Ice Trap

## Description

Ice Trap is an ice spell that creates an AOE slow and root effect using ice, immobilizing enemies in place.

## Mechanics

- **Effect**: AOE slow and root using ice
- **Type**: Ice spell
- **Result**: Immobilization

## Usage

Used by [[Mimi]] and ice mages of the [[Cult of Elements]] to control enemy movement and prevent escapes.
