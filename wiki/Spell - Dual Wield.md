# Spell: Dual Wield

## Description

Dual Wield is a passive ability that allows the user to fight with two katanas simultaneously, doubling offensive capability.

## Mechanics

- **Effect**: Uses two katanas to fight
- **Type**: Passive
- **Weapon**: Dual katanas

## Usage

Possessed by [[Ygor]] and samurai of the [[Cult of Fayth]] who have mastered fighting with a blade in each hand.
