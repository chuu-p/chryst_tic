# Cult of Gold Great Academies

The prestigious academies of the Cult of Gold, where alteration mages are trained in the arts of material transformation and transmutation. These institutions serve as the intellectual and magical centers for Tymo's people and the Cult's transactional magic system.

## Description

Grand halls of learning characterized by laboratories, alchemical workshops, and vast libraries of material science. The architecture is elegant and refined, reflecting the Cult's focus on wealth, precision, and transformation. Gold filigree adorns the buildings, and the air smells of ozone and precious metals being transmuted.

## Lore

The Cult of Gold accesses the alteration field - they are purely transactional mages capable of changing materials and utilizing alteration magic. Their Great Academies represent the pursuit of knowledge, wealth, and material perfection. Tymo's skills as an inventor (creating guns, graphene-weave cloaks, and kinetic metamaterials) stem from this tradition. The academies' philosophy contrasts with the Cult of Fayth - where Fayth seeks unity and compassion, Gold seeks individual advancement through material mastery.
