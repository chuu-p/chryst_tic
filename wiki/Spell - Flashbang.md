# Spell: Flashbang

## Description

Flashbang is an ice spell that compresses water to create a blinding flash of light, disorienting enemies in combat.

## Mechanics

- **Effect**: Blinding flash of light via water compression
- **Type**: Ice spell
- **Result**: Temporary blindness

## Usage

Used by [[Mimi]] and ice mages of the [[Cult of Elements]] to create tactical openings by blinding enemies with compressed water bursts.
