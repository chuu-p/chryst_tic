# Spell: Healing Hands

## Description

Healing Hands is a holy spell that heals a person by laying on hands and transferring Fayth energy directly into their body.

## Mechanics

- **Effect**: Heals wounds through physical touch
- **Type**: Holy spell
- **Method**: Laying on hands, Fayth transfer

## Usage

Used by [[Chryst]] and healers of the [[Cult of Fayth]] to mend injuries through direct physical contact and divine energy transfer.
