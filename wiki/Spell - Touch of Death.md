# Spell: Touch of Death

## Description

Touch of Death is a shadow spell that kills anyone with a single touch, but at the cost of the caster's own life force.

## Mechanics

- **Effect**: Instant death on touch
- **Type**: Shadow spell
- **Cost**: Caster's life force

## Usage

Used by [[Berlynde]] and assassins of the [[Cult of Maxt]] as a last resort when the caster is willing to pay the ultimate price.
