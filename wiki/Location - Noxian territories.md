# Noxian Territories

The oppressive, highly structured military regime where Zael grew up. Noxus is a violently practical authoritarian state that values strength, discipline, and absolute obedience above all else, producing some of the most skilled combatants in the world.

## Description

The Noxian territories are characterized by massive fortresses, strict military discipline, and an atmosphere of constant surveillance and control. The architecture is harsh and functional - grey stone barracks, training grounds, and weapon manufactories. The regime demands absolute loyalty and crushes individuality.

## Lore

Noxus represents the antithesis of freedom - a place where rules are absolute and individual will is crushed. Zael grew up here, learning to survive by exploiting the system before defecting to become a masterful, rule-breaking rebel. When Chryst establishes his magical academy and chooses the "Open Source Arc," Zael joins as his most talented but insubordinate student. The Noxian philosophy of "strength above all" directly contrasts with the Cult of Fayth's radical compassion.
