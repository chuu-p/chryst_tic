# Spell: Holy Tattoos

## Description

Holy Tattoos are passive markings that act as executable code, glowing when active to improve the efficiency of spell casting.

## Mechanics

- **Effect**: Executable tattoos that boost casting efficiency
- **Type**: Passive
- **Visual**: Glowing tattoos when casting

## Usage

Possessed by [[Chryst]] and advanced members of the [[Cult of Fayth]] who have inscribed divine code directly onto their bodies for faster spell execution.
