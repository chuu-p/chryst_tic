# Brsykyr Occupational Mayor's Office

The seat of Maxt authority in Brsykyr, where Chryst and Astryd (then Axtritt) were brought before the Mayor. This is where Astryd was dishonorably discharged and Chryst made the wager to win tournament recognition for the Cult of Fayth.

## Description

A cold, dimly lit room that smells of old parchment and sharp iron. The office is furnished with brutalist Maxt furniture - rigid, functional, and devoid of comfort. The Mayor's desk is piled with documents of occupation, and the atmosphere is oppressive with the weight of absolute authority.

## Lore

This office represents the bureaucratic face of Maxt occupation. The Mayor embodies the Maxt value system where strength is the only currency of respect. His wager with Chryst - that the Cult of Fayth could be recognized if Chryst won the tournament - set the primary conflict for the entire Brsykyr chapter. The office's cold authoritarianism contrasted sharply with Chryst's calm, resonant faith.
