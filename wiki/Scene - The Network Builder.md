# Scene: The Network Builder

## Scene Configuration
* **Location**: Svalbaard Island - The Frozen Heart Storage Facility deep within a cleared dungeon. 
* **Characters Involved**: [[Chryst]], [[Tymo]], [[Berlynde]], several scribe-priests of the Cult of Fayth.
* **Key Motivation**: Chryst must establish the core routing protocols to balance the voltage levels of the expanding Fayth network before it collapses under its own weight.

## Structure

### Beginning
Before they can even reach the master storage node, Chryst, [[Tymo]], and [[Berlynde]] must clear the sprawling, frost-bitten dungeon of Svalbaard's ruins. The structural magic of the ruins resists Chryst's golden winds, forcing the party to rely on their distinct skills—Tymo utilizes highly concentrated geometric explosives through his alteration alchemy, whilst Berlynde directs the skirmishes with flawless analog tactical precision. During a brutal ambush by subterranean monstrosities, Chryst's magic momentarily fails him; he only survives because he trusts Berlynde's covering fire and Tymo's blast shields. It is a profound lesson for Chryst: he cannot bear the weight of the mission entirely on his own magic without collapsing.

### Middle
Having successfully breached the final vault, they arrive deep in the freezing subterranean caverns of Svalbaard. Chryst is surrounded by towering racks of icy gem enclosures—the Frozen Hearts of passed believers. The chamber acts as the ultimate overvolting protection and mass Fayth storage for his holy power grid. A squad of scribe-priests labor intensely, carving prayer syntax into the ice. Chryst is meticulously analyzing the flow of the "golden winds" acting as network traffic passing through the chamber.

### Middle
The network is expanding too fast as new cities are converted. The scribes report that the message passing between the [[Bysfrost]] node and the newly established [[Brsykyr]] node is causing massive latency, leading to localized power failures where defensive protective auras are dropping in vulnerable villages.

Chryst enters a trancelike state, visually tracking the golden arcs of light bouncing between the Frozen Hearts. He realizes the linear array structure of their prayers is fundamentally flawed for a network of this scale. The magic field operates like electricity—it requires intelligent load balancing.

In a sequence of intense, rapid engineering, Chryst begins rewriting the foundational holy code. He carves new, conditional loops directly into the central altar's master Frozen Heart: `if (node_voltage > max_threshold) { route(overflow, svalbaard_battery); }`. The scribes watch in horror and awe as he actively modifies the divine scriptures of [[Frydemann]] in real-time, risking absolute heresy for the sake of efficiency.

### Climax & Ending
Chryst executes the new prayer. The entire cavern dims for a terrifying "almost works" moment, the hum dropping to a deadly silence. The scribe-priests hold their breath, fearing the network has fatally crashed. 

Then, a perfectly resonant, synchronized hum vibrates through the cavern. The golden winds re-ignite, flowing smoothly and beautifully across the arrays of frozen hearts like a meticulously optimized circuit. The network holds. 

Chryst steps back, emotionally drained but triumphant. This breakthrough means the network can scale indefinitely—bringing him one massive step closer to generating enough Fayth to finally unfreeze his brother’s soul. But a lingering shot of a single, cracking Frozen Heart in the back of the room implies the structural cost of his obsession.
