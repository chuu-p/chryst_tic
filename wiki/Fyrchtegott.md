# Fyrchtegott

Fyrchtegott is the democratically elected Village Priest of [[Yswyndt]], taking the mantle long after the legendary [[Frydemann]] abandoned the monastery. Despite belonging to the eerie and ominous [[Cult of Death]], the people of Yswyndt harbor no prejudice; they accept him entirely because of his authentic ability to heal and bless the community, regardless of the dark, voodoo-like source of his magic.

## Background
Historically, the Cult of Death operated in the shadows—a macabre school utilizing heavy smoke magic and necromantic aesthetics to enact their faith. However, Fyrchtegott proved that death is simply another mechanism of the world. After assuming the role of Village Priest, one of his primary duties became the training of apprentice priests within the icy confines of the Yswyndt Monastery. 

He acts as the harsh, pragmatic mentor to [[Chryst|Helfrych]] (the boy who would become [[Chryst]]). However, their relationship is fundamentally detached. Chryst is intensely self-reliant, choosing his own curriculum and relying on Fyrchtegott merely for oversight and guidance into the forbidden [[Holy Language]]. At the same time, Chryst's brother, [[Cosymo]], was educated entirely separately by a different mentor dedicated to training hero apprentices, structurally separating the brothers' foundations before they eventually merged.

## Appearance
Gaunt, severe, and shrouded in an ever-present, cloying gloom. Fyrchtegott wears threadbare clerical robes lined with charred bone amulets and fetishes—heavy nods to his Cult of Death roots. A thick, sweet-smelling smoke constantly billows from the censers chained to his belt, masking the freezing, metallic scent of the Frost-Archives. His eyes are described as reflecting the pale blue of the deep North, completely unfeeling and piercing. His hands are permanently black from frostbite and the corrosive magical ink used to draft unholy syntax.

## Personality
Cold as the ice that surrounds him, heavily cynical, and profoundly pragmatic. He has stripped away the comforting illusions of orthodox religion, preferring the agonizing truth of mechanism. He speaks with a voice like grinding stones and brooks no weakness, viewing emotion as a syntactical error in the code of reality. To him, death is not a tragedy, but a debt that must inevitably be paid.

### GOALS
To fulfill his elected duty to Yswyndt, train the next generation of priests, and quietly oversee Chryst's manipulation of the divine code, ensuring the boy's power is sharp and mechanically flawless.

### RELATIONSHIPS
- **[[Chryst]]**: He mentors Chryst, but they lack a father-son connection. Both accept this emotional distance. He views Chryst as a highly capable, self-reliant instrument—a sharpened blade capable of rewriting flawed reality. 
- **[[Frydemann]]**: He holds a bitter respect for Frydemann’s genius, but resents the heavy structural burden the man orphaned when he fled Yswyndt.

### LIFESTYLE
Punishingly ascetic. He spends his days managing the village's spiritual needs, but retreats to the freezing Frost-Archives at night, surrounded by toxic, never-freezing ink, forbidden vellum, and the thick smoke of his dark magic.

### PRESENTATION
Intimidating, entirely detached, and ominously still. He merges with the shadows and the smoke of the library, often appearing as though the archives themselves are speaking.

### DIALOGUE
"In my land, we do not ask if the blade is holy. We ask if it is sharp. The Goddess set the rules; she left it to us to hack them."
