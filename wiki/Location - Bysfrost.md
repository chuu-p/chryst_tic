# Bysfrost

A snowy, desperate village in the northern reaches of Norholdt, rotting from the inside with poverty and moral decay brought on by the war. This is where Chryst establishes his first Cult of Fayth mission after departing his homeland. The village exists under the shadow of Maxt "protection" but remains free from occupation.

## Description

Bysfrost is a weathered settlement of timber and stone, perpetually dusted with snow. The village exudes an atmosphere heavy with the weight of ongoing war and economic despair. Despite its condition, it serves as a crucial foothold for Chryst's mission to spread the Fayth network across the continent.

## Lore

Bysfrost represents Chryst's transition from lone wanderer to spiritual leader. It is here that his methodology becomes chillingly utilitarian - he begins viewing villagers not as people to save, but as tactical "anchors" and network nodes for his Fayth network. The village's poverty and desperation make its people receptive to Chryst's message of radical compassion and unity.
