# Spell: Phantom Phobia

## Description

Phantom Phobia is a shadow spell that extracts a target's deepest fear and overlays it onto their allies, spreading terror through shared hallucination.

## Mechanics

- **Effect**: Extracts and spreads deepest fears
- **Type**: Shadow spell
- **Target**: Target and their allies

## Usage

Used by [[Berlynde]] and fear mages of the [[Cult of Maxt]] to break enemy morale by weaponizing their own psychological vulnerabilities.
