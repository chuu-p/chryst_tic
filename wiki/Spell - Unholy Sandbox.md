# Spell: Unholy Sandbox

## Description

Unholy Sandbox is an unholy spell that isolates a reality pocket where moral laws are suspended, allowing unrestricted blood magic and forbidden practices.

## Mechanics

- **Effect**: Isolates a reality pocket with suspended moral laws
- **Type**: Unholy spell
- **Environment**: Blood magic allowed

## Usage

Used by [[Chryst]] and dark practitioners of the [[Cult of Fayth]] to create safe spaces for performing otherwise forbidden magical experiments.
