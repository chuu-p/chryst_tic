# Frydemann

Frydemann is a living legend within the world, the original architect of the "Bible" program, and arguably the most powerful mage to have ever lived.

## Background

In his prime, Frydemann encoded the core scriptures of the Cult of Fayth. He recognized that prayer arrays, structured correctly, functioned as source code that directly manipulated the golden winds of Fayth. He alone foresaw the true potential of this: a global, interconnected spiritual network that could systematically eradicate human suffering by processing Fayth energy on an industrial scale.

However, the realization of what would be required of him—to dedicate his entire mortal existence to being the eternal custodian and messiah of this grand machine—terrified him. The crown was simply too heavy. Opting for a life of freedom over a life of eternal martyrdom, Frydemann abandoned his creation.

### Current Life

Today, he resides in the opulent, decentralized merchant city of Byzantine. Living in immense luxury, he serves as a highly paid advisor to the city's elites. He spends his days indulging in fine wines, exotic substances, and philosophical detachment, trying his best to forget the immense spiritual responsibility he orphaned.

## Appearance

Though decades have passed since his prime, Frydemann retains an aura of immense, quiet power. He dresses in loose, silken robes of vibrant colors—a stark contrast to the austere grey of the Fayth clergy. His eyes are perpetually weary, half-lidded with the haze of luxurious narcotics, but they flash with sharp, uncomfortable intellect when prodded.

## Personality

Cynical, hedonistic, but tragically self-aware. He possesses a bitter wisdom. He tells those who seek his guidance that they take magic "too seriously," urging them to just enjoy the flow of life rather than trying to engineer it. Deep down, he is masking the profound guilt of a god who walked away from his universe.

# CHARACTER MAP

CHARACTER NAME: Frydemann

## ORIGIN OF URGE

When he stood at the precipice of godhood, he looked down at the crushing weight of a billion souls crying out for his protection. He realized that to save them, he would have to become a machine. He chose his humanity over their salvation.

## CORE URGE

**To escape the burden of his own potential.**
In order to survive and get my needs met, I must numb myself to the world's suffering and convince myself that I am just a man.

## STATED BELIEF (LIE)

Magic is just a tool, not a religion. The world will sort itself out; it doesn’t need a savior, and it certainly doesn't need me.

## THE CORE URGE AFFECTS ALL AREAS OF THEIR LIFE

### GOALS

To live comfortably, forget the past, and avoid any entanglements with the Cult of Fayth.

### RELATIONSHIPS

Superficial and transient. He enjoys the company of sycophants and merchants because they want his mind, not his soul.

### LIFESTYLE

Decadent, indulgent, and intentionally directionless.

### PRESENTATION

A flamboyant, eccentric retired genius who acts more foolish than he truly is.

### DIALOGUE

"You’re putting too much pressure on yourself, kid. It’s just magic. Look at that woman selling apples—she doesn't care about your holy arrays. Stop trying to carry the sky. Have a drink."
