# Spell: Artillery Mage

## Description

Artillery Mage is an ice spell that casts high AOE damage from a distance using ice shards, functioning as a magical artillery unit.

## Mechanics

- **Effect**: High AOE damage from distance using ice shards
- **Type**: Ice spell
- **Range**: Long distance

## Usage

Used by [[Mimi]] and ice mages of the [[Cult of Elements]] to bombard enemies from safe distances with explosive ice projectiles.
