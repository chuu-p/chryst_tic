# Syrvann

Tymo's decentralized homeland, a country known for wandering nomadic hunter tribes and vast icy forests. Unlike the structured kingdoms around it, Syrvann has no central authority, instead relying on nomadic tribes that follow ancient migration patterns through the wilderness.

## Description

Syrvann is a sprawling landscape of icy forests, frozen lakes, and untouched wilderness. The country has no grand cities or centralized government - instead, nomadic hunter tribes move through the forests following game and seasonal patterns. The people are deeply connected to nature, practicing a philosophy of patience and inner freedom (_Sisu_).

## Lore

Syrvann represents the ideal of freedom without authority - a place where people are happy despite hardship because they choose their own path. The country was invaded and occupied by the Maxt Empire, with many inhabitants (like Tymo) taken as slaves. Tymo's ultimate goal is to liberate his homeland, and his character arc revolves around choosing peaceful revolution over vengeful warfare. The ancestral halfling engineers of Syrvann even designed the infrastructure for the Frozen Hell Dungeon.
