# Spell: Banish Undead

## Description

Banish Undead is a holy spell that banishes or outright kills undead creatures through the application of purifying holy magic.

## Mechanics

- **Effect**: Banishes or destroys undead
- **Type**: Holy spell
- **Target**: Undead creatures only

## Usage

Used by [[Chryst]] and exorcists of the [[Cult of Fayth]] to cleanse areas of undead threats and protect the living from corruption.
