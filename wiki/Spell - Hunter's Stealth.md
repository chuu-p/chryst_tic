# Spell: Hunter's Stealth

## Description

Hunter's Stealth is a passive ability that allows the user to set traps and turn invisible after preparation, enabling ambush tactics.

## Mechanics

- **Effect**: Sets traps, turns invisible after preparation
- **Type**: Passive
- **Tactic**: Ambush preparation

## Usage

Possessed by [[Tymo]] and stealth hunters of the [[Cult of Gold]] who prepare kill zones and then vanish from sight.
