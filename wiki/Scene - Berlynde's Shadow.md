### The Smog of War

The air was a thick sludge of oil and iron, but Helfrych didn't need to look to know where his brother was. He could feel Cosymo’s pulse in the air, a steady rhythm against the chaotic percussion of the MaxT rifles.

"Heave!" Helfrych barked.

He surged upward, the golden winds of fayth swirling around his hands to create a momentary barrier. Cosymo didn't hesitate. He dived under the shield, his short sword carving a path through the Maxt vanguard with a precision born of years of shared practice.

Across the cratered field, [[Berlynde]] watched, her black plate armor reflecting the fires of the burning trenches. Her men stood several paces behind her, their eyes wide with a fear that wasn't for the enemy, but for the woman they served.

"Your coordination is impressive, little priests," Berlynde’s voice came through her helm, cold and hollow. "But teamwork is a crutch for the weak. I have no such need."

She swung the Obsidian Aegis. A wave of shadow rolled across the mud, extinguishing the golden light of Helfrych’s shield. Her own soldiers scrambled to get out of the way, knowing she wouldn't hesitate to cut through them to reach her target.

"She’s alone!" Cosymo shouted, mud spraying as he pivoted. "She has no one to watch her back!"

They moved like a single organism. Helfrych blinded her with a flash of divine light while Cosymo struck at the joints of her armor. For a moment, the unstoppable general actually stumbled. She looked back at her men, but they didn't move to aid her. Their loyalty was bought with coin and fear, neither of which mattered in the heat of a losing duel.

"I do not need them," Berlynde hissed, her voice dripping with sudden, desperate malice. "I only need one touch."

She dropped her guard, a move so reckless Helfrych hesitated. Her gauntlet began to bleed a darkness so deep it swallowed the very light of the explosions around them.

"The Touch of Death," Helfrych whispered, his heart turning to ice. Fyrchtegott had spoken of this—a shadow-link that consumed both the caster and the victim.

Berlynde lunged. Her hand, wreathed in the killing shadow, reached for Helfrych’s throat. Helfrych stood frozen, the golden winds around him faltering under the weight of the void.

_Thump._

A body slammed into him, knocking him back into the bloody slush of the trench.

Helfrych blinked. Cosymo was between them.

Berlynde’s hand was pressed firmly against Cosymo’s chest. The shadow poured into him like ink into water, turning his veins black and his eyes to hollow pits of ash. Berlynde gasped, her own armor cracking as the spell took its price from her soul, but she didn't let go.

"Cosymo!" Helfrych screamed.

Cosymo didn't fall. He grabbed Berlynde’s arm, his grip tightening even as his skin withered. He looked back at Helfrych, a final, unshakeable trust in his dying eyes.

"Do it, brother," Cosymo wheezed. "Rewrite the rules."

Berlynde pulled back, her shadow-exhausted form stumbling into the fog as her men finally rushed forward to retrieve her.

Helfrych was left in the mud, clutching his brother's darkening body. The holy magic was useless here. The Goddess was silent.

He reached into the hidden folds of his robes, touching the unholy scripts.

"I will," Helfrych whispered, the mercury light beginning to bleed into his eyes. "I will decide who dies today."
