# Scene: A Broken Knight's Grace

The sun rises over a scarred [[Beisfrost]]. While the villagers are safe, the atmosphere is far from celebratory. The [[Beisfrost Mayor]] and the local townsfolk gather around the injured [[Astryd]], but instead of offering thanks, they berate her for her incompetence. They mock the "great nation of [[Maxt]]" for sending a knight who couldn't even handle a band of bandits without the help of a wandering stranger.

Astryd is mentally broken. In her culture, strength and martial prowess are everything. To be saved by a man from [[Yswyndt]]—the very land her people are currently conquering—is a source of deep shame. She feels she has failed her nation and her identity.

[[Chryst]] intervenes once more, but this time with words instead of shields. He speaks of redemption and the fact that everyone can be led astray. He tells her that while she failed in her duty of force, she has been given a chance to find a new path through good deeds.

NOTE: Astryd asks him, now that he saved her life, if he now also wants to save her dignity. He says no, he just follows the way his goddess leads him. Astryd is pretty depressed now. Her arc will become to regain her faith in herself and develop an individual sense of worth, not tied to her identity as a knight of her nation, [[Maxt]]. 

Seeing the village's need for hope and direction, Chryst asks the Mayor for permission to establish a community of the [[Cult of Fayth]]. Moved by Chryst's actions during the raid, the Mayor agrees, giving Chryst the space to build his first church.

SUMMARY
The villagers mock [[Astryd]] for her failure, leaving her humiliated. [[Chryst]] comforts her with a message of redemption and gains permission from the [[Beisfrost Mayor]] to establish a church.
