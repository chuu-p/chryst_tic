# Spell: Hunter's Senses

## Description

Hunter's Senses is a passive ability that grants elite perception to detect ambushes and sense danger before it manifests.

## Mechanics

- **Effect**: Elite perception, ambush detection
- **Type**: Passive
- **Range**: Enhanced awareness

## Usage

Possessed by [[Tymo]] and hunters of the [[Cult of Gold]] who need to remain vigilant against surprise attacks in dangerous environments.
