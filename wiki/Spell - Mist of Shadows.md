# Spell: Mist of Shadows

## Description

Mist of Shadows is a shadow spell that envelops the area in magical darkness, leaving no trace of the caster's presence or passage.

## Mechanics

- **Effect**: Area-enveloping magical darkness
- **Type**: Shadow spell
- **Special**: Leaves no trace

## Usage

Used by [[Berlynde]] and shadow mages of the [[Cult of Maxt]] to create cover for stealth operations and disappear from sight.
