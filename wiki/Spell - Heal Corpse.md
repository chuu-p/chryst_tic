# Spell: Heal Corpse

## Description

Heal Corpse is an unholy spell that heals a corpse by laying on hands, restoring physical integrity to dead tissue through Fayth manipulation.

## Mechanics

- **Effect**: Repairs dead tissue on corpses
- **Type**: Unholy spell
- **Method**: Laying on hands

## Usage

Used by [[Chryst]] and necromancers of the [[Cult of Fayth]] to preserve or restore corpses for reanimation or soul compilation rituals.
