# Spell: Holy Blade

## Description

Holy Blade is a holy spell that creates a sword carved with holy programming runes which judges its victims. The blade bounces off those pure of heart, only striking the unworthy.

## Mechanics

- **Effect**: Creates a rune-carved sword that judges targets
- **Type**: Holy spell
- **Special**: Bounces off pure-hearted individuals

## Usage

Used by [[Chryst]] and paladins of the [[Cult of Fayth]] as a self-judging weapon that only harms those deemed impure or sinful.
