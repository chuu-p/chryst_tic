# The Unnamed General

The Unnamed General is a high-ranking military commander within the [[Macht]] (MaxT) Empire. He is a primary antagonist during the Hamburg chapter. He represents the sheer force of pure, analog, industrial power in stark contrast to Chryst's digital, ethereal Fayth network.

## Background

The General is a decorated veteran of the MaxT expansion campaigns into the Nordic territories. Years ago, he rescued [[Berlynde]] from a perilous situation, forming a complicated bond with her. Although Berlynde eventually quit the military, the General still views her as an asset—or perhaps a daughter-figure—that rightfully belongs by his side.

When Chryst and his crew arrive in Hamburg (the MaxT capital), the General sees Chryst not merely as a rebel, but as an ideological plague. He recognizes the Fayth network for what it is: a decentralized threat to the strict, centralized order of the Empire.

### Philosophy: Analog Power vs Digital Magic

The General is fiercely "Anti-Fayth." He distrusts the invisible, ethereal nature of Chryst's magic. To the General, true power is tangible: raw iron, steam engines, gunpowder, and the sweat of disciplined soldiers. He believes that relying on the "golden winds" makes people soft and dependent. If someone unplugs the network, they are powerless; but a steel blade never loses its edge.

## Appearance

Imposing, built like a brick wall, and heavily scorched by years of warfare. He eschews the fine, shadow-mage silks typical of Maxt high command in favor of heavy, functional plate armor augmented with crude steam-pistons to enhance his physical strength. His face is heavily scarred, bearing a stern, unyielding expression.

## Personality

Brutal, highly disciplined, and coldly rational. He is not a mustache-twirling villain; he simply believes his way is the only way humanity survives the dark. He respects strength and despises those who hide behind "miracles."

# CHARACTER MAP

CHARACTER NAME: The Unnamed General

## ORIGIN OF URGE

Having watched magic inevitably fail on the battlefield when the mana ran dry or the gods fell silent, he learned that only physical engineering and human grit endure.

## CORE URGE

**To crush the ethereal and establish absolute, tangible order.**
In order to survive and get my needs met, I must rely solely on what I can build, touch, and break with my own hands.

## STATED BELIEF (LIE)

Magic is a crutch for the weak. When the lights go out, only iron remains.

## THE CORE URGE AFFECTS ALL AREAS OF THEIR LIFE

### GOALS

To crush Chryst’s rebellion, separate Berlynde from what he sees as a "cult," and prove the supremacy of Maxt engineering over Fayth miracles.

### RELATIONSHIPS

Authoritarian. He views his soldiers as cogs in a beautiful machine. His attachment to Berlynde is genuine but deeply controlling.

### LIFESTYLE

Spartan and militaristic. He sleeps in barracks even when offered palaces.

### PRESENTATION

A walking dreadnought of steel and steam.

### DIALOGUE

"Your god is just a current in the air, boy. What happens when I build a wall so thick the wind can't blow through it? You bleed, just like the rest."
