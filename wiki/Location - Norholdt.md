# Norholdt

A Nordic Kingdom whose southern half, including cities like Brsykyr and Ofyo, is currently occupied by the Maxt Empire. The northern city of Bysfrost remains free but is under imminent threat. Norholdt represents the struggle of Nordic independence against industrial imperialism.

## Description

Norholdt is a mountainous Nordic region characterized by deep fjords, dense forests, and brutal winters. The kingdom is divided - its southern regions under Maxt occupation feature brutalist architecture and military presence, while the north maintains traditional Nordic culture with scattered free settlements like Bysfrost.

## Lore

Norholdt embodies the central conflict of the story - the ancient, communal Nordic way of life versus the industrial, authoritarian might of the Maxt Empire. The region's occupation has created a culture of resistance and desperation, making its people receptive to Chryst's message of unity through the Fayth network. The eventual liberation of Norholdt becomes a key goal for characters like Tymo and Astryd.
