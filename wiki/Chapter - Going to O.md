# Chapter: Going to Ofyo

## Summary

[[Chryst]], [[Astryd]], and the freed [[Syrvann]] slave [[Tymo]] begin their journey south toward the [[Maxt Capital City]] through the unforgiving northern wilderness. The trio forms a tense, professional dynamic: Chryst drives them forward with relentless purpose, Astryd serves as the group's shield and moral anchor, and Tymo—against all odds—becomes the glue that holds them together.

Tymo, a [[halfling]] alteration mage and follower of the [[Cult of Gold]] (alteration mage = Priest of the Cult of Gold), brings practical survival skills the others lack: he can set camp in a blizzard, track game through whiteout conditions, and sense danger before it strikes. More importantly, he teaches Chryst the one thing his obsession has blinded him to—patience. When Chryst demands they push through a three-day storm, Tymo simply sits down in the snow and says, "The weather doesn't care about your mission. We walk ten kilometers today, or we don't walk at all."

During the journey, Chryst experiments with a new method of connecting believers to the Fayth network: small iron crosses inscribed with [[Holy Language]] script. When a believer holds the cross and prays, their thoughts and emotions flow directly into the network. Chryst discovers something unexpected when Astryd prays—he can feel her pain, her guilt, and the quiet devotion she carries for him. The intimacy of the connection unsettles him. For the first time, the network isn't just a tool; it's a window into the people he's supposed to lead.

Tymo and Chryst develop a professional respect that borders on friendship without either of them acknowledging it. Tymo helps Chryst produce holy technology—carved rune stones, mass-producible prayer crosses, and early prototypes of what will eventually become a portable scripture-writing device. In exchange, Chryst teaches Tymo that faith isn't just a power source; it's a framework for meaning. Tymo, who joined the journey to gain strength for liberating his homeland, begins to understand that liberation requires more than power—it requires something worth liberating for.

The chapter culminates in their arrival at the outskirts of the [[Maxt Capital City]], where the scale of the empire becomes undeniable: a sprawling metropolis of iron, steam, and surveillance. Chryst realizes that spreading the Cult of Fayth here will require not just faith, but strategy.

## Character Arcs

- **[[Chryst]]**: Begins to experience the Fayth network as emotional connection rather than raw power. The cross-prayer experiment with Astryd plants the first seed of doubt about his purely utilitarian approach—he can feel what his followers feel, and it complicates his calculations.
- **[[Astryd]]**: Finds her role within the trio. No longer a Maxt knight or a Brsykyr priestess, she becomes something new: Chryst's shield, yes, but also the person who keeps him grounded in human connection.
- **[[Tymo]]**: Transitions from a freed slave seeking power to a true believer in something larger than himself. His patience becomes the group's survival mechanism, and his technical genius begins to transform Chryst's abstract vision into physical reality.
