# Spell: Resist Magic

## Description

Resist Magic is a passive ability that grants high resistance to all schools of magic, making the user extremely difficult to affect with spells.

## Mechanics

- **Effect**: High resistance to all schools of magic
- **Type**: Passive
- **Protection**: Universal magic resistance

## Usage

Possessed by [[Styllfried]] and void adepts who have trained their bodies and minds to naturally resist magical effects.
