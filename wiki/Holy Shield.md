# Spell: Holy Shield

**Holy Shield** (commonly known by commoners as a "**bubble**") is a fundamental divine spell of the [[Cult of Fayth]].

## Description

The spell manifests a shimmering, translucent sphere of divine energy around the target. This shield absorbs and negates all incoming physical and magical damage for a short duration.

## Mechanics

- **Effect**: Complete damage immunity.
- **Duration**: Short.
- **Visual**: A glowing, golden "bubble" or sphere.

## Usage

It is often used by priests of the [[Cult of Fayth]] to protect themselves or others during physical altercations or battle. However, under [[Maxt]] law, the use of such magic by unauthorized individuals is strictly regulated and can lead to severe legal penalties.
