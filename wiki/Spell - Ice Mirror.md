# Spell: Ice Mirror

## Description

Ice Mirror is an ice spell that creates mirrors of ice which reflect light and bewilder foes, causing confusion and misdirection.

## Mechanics

- **Effect**: Creates ice mirrors that reflect light and bewilder foes
- **Type**: Ice spell
- **Result**: Confusion and misdirection

## Usage

Used by [[Mimi]] and illusion mages of the [[Cult of Elements]] to disorient enemies with reflective surfaces and light manipulation.
