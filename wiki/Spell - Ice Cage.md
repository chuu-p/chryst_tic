# Spell: Ice Cage

## Description

Ice Cage is an ice spell that encases the caster in an invulnerable ice tomb while damaging surrounding enemies.

## Mechanics

- **Effect**: Self-encasement in invulnerable ice, damages surrounding enemies
- **Type**: Ice spell
- **Defense**: Complete invulnerability

## Usage

Used by [[Mimi]] and ice mages of the [[Cult of Elements]] as a desperate defensive measure that also punishes nearby enemies.
