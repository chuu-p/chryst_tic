SUMMARY - Chryst is trying to link a new congregation into the broader Fayth network by installing a highly potent Frozen Heart crystal to act as a localized battery and relay. As the "Sunday Pulse" hits, the influx is too powerful, bringing with it the raw, unfiltered emotions of thousands of worshippers across the entire network—their pain, their starvation, and their desperation. Overwhelmed, Chryst violently lashes out at Tymo, nearly destroying the church. He then explains his intense psychic burden to Tymo, forging a solemn understanding between them.

## Scene Configuration
* **Location**: A small, dilapidated Cult of Fayth chapel in one of the occupied cities during a Sunday Service.
* **Characters Involved**: [[Chryst]], [[Tymo]], several local worshippers.
* **Key Motivation**: Chryst is trying to link this new congregation into the broader Fayth network by installing a highly potent Frozen Heart crystal to act as a localized battery and relay.

## Structure

### Beginning
The scene opens with an eerie calm. It is Sunday, the designated day when the Fayth network receives its massive weekly "pulse" of energy. Chryst is standing at the altar, eyes closed, hands tracing the glowing, golden runes inscribed in the air. The local worshippers are kneeling, murmuring the holy source code laid out in the Bible. Tymo watches from the shadows, skeptical but intrigued by the raw power humming in the room.

### Middle
As the clock strikes noon, the "Sunday Pulse" hits. The church is filled with blinding golden winds, rushing through the rafters. But the influx is too powerful. Chryst’s second, frozen heart beats a violent, singular drum beat. Instantly, Chryst is not just flooded with energy, but with the raw, unfiltered emotions of thousands of worshippers across the entire network—their pain, their starvation, and their desperation. 

Chryst staggers. He attempts to route the excess voltage into the new Frozen Heart reliquary, working fast to script "if/else" cooling loops. Tymo, noticing the structural beams beginning to warp from the intense magical heat, steps forward, grabbing Chryst’s arm and yelling over the roar of the winds to cut the connection before the church explodes.

Overwhelmed and emotionally compromised by the psychic weight of the network, Chryst violently lashes out, throwing Tymo across the pews with a blast of force that fractures the altar. The golden light stutters, then stabilizes just below the critical threshold. It almost worked smoothly, but failed at the last second, nearly costing them the congregation.

### Climax & Ending
The winds die down to a gentle, humming glow. The worshippers, unhurt but terrified, look at Chryst as a wrathful god. Tymo slowly gets up, wiping blood from his jaw, furious but waiting for an explanation.

Chryst drops to his knees, clutching his chest. In a rare break from his stoic, professional demeanor, he turns to Tymo in the empty vestibule. He breathes heavily and explains—in a deeply vulnerable state—that on Sundays, he isn't just a conduit for power. He feels *everything*. The collective suffering of the oppressed Nordic states floods his mind at once, and trying to control his dead brother's corrupted soul matrix during the pulse stretches his sanity to its absolute limit. Tymo, realizing the agonizing burden Chryst carries, nods slowly. The tension remains, but a quiet, solemn understanding is forged between them.
