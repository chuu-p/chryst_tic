# Spell: Phase Traps

## Description

Phase Traps is an alteration spell that depletes ground density, causing enemies to sink into rock before the ground solidifies, trapping them.

## Mechanics

- **Effect**: Ground density depletion, sinking enemies, solidification
- **Type**: Alteration spell
- **Result**: Enemies trapped in stone

## Usage

Used by [[Tymo]] and trap mages of the [[Cult of Gold]] to immobilize enemies by turning the ground beneath them into quicksand-like substance.
