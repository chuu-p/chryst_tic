# Spell: Lend Invoke

## Description

Lend Invoke is a holy spell that channels life energy from one person into another through shared prayer, transferring vitality between willing participants.

## Mechanics

- **Effect**: Transfers life energy between individuals
- **Type**: Holy spell
- **Method**: Shared prayer

## Usage

Used by [[Chryst]] and healers of the [[Cult of Fayth]] to donate life force to those in need through communal prayer rituals.
