# Spell: Splash Damage

## Description

Splash Damage is an ice spell that creates an ice explosion harming all enemies in the area, dealing damage to everything within range.

## Mechanics

- **Effect**: Ice explosion with area damage
- **Type**: Ice spell
- **Area**: AOE damage

## Usage

Used by [[Mimi]] and ice mages of the [[Cult of Elements]] to clear groups of enemies with explosive ice blasts.
