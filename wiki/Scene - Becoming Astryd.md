The night in Brsykyr was as cold as a sharpening stone, and the moon reflected in the still waters of the ancient stone fountain like a silver eye. [[Chryst]] stood by the water, his shadow long and thin against the wet cobbles. Beside him stood Axtritt, her armor discarded, her shoulders bare to the biting frost.

"The life you knew is a husk, Axtritt," Chryst said, his voice a warm hum in the frigid air. "The legion, the ranks, the service to Maxt—they were chains that bound you to a world of blood. To follow the Fayth is to let go of the shore. You must let the waters take the ghost of who you were."

He placed his hands on her shoulders. They were warm, a stark contrast to the freezing night. Axtritt looked at the black water, her reflection trembling.

"I have been a weapon for so long," she whispered, her voice cracking. "I do not know how to be a person again."

"You will not be a person. You will be a light," Chryst replied. He guided her into the fountain until the water reached her heart. "Let the ice enter your veins and freeze the sins of the past. When you rise, you shall be tempered by the cold and reborn in the fire of the Megami's love."

He pressed her back, the dark water closing over her head. For a moment, there was only the silence of the deep. Then, with a gasp, she emerged, the moonlight clinging to her like a shroud.

"Your old name drowned," Chryst declared, his eyes burning with a fierce compassion. "You are reborn. You are Astryd. You are the shield of the Fayth, and you shall never walk alone in the dark again."

Astryd looked at him, and for the first time in years, the hollow ache in her chest was gone. She reached out and touched his hand, a silent promise between them that transcended any oath of service.

SUMMARY
In a secluded area of Brsykyr, [[Chryst]] baptizes Axtritt in a freezing fountain. She lets go of her old identity as a [[Maxt]] soldier and is reborn as [[Astryd]], a devoted follower of the [[Cult of Fayth]]. This scene marks a deep romantic and spiritual bond between them.
