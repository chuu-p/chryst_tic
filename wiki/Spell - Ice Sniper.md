# Spell: Ice Sniper

## Description

Ice Sniper is an ice spell and ultimate ability that charges a massive beam of light dealing huge line damage to everything in its path.

## Mechanics

- **Effect**: Massive beam of light, huge line damage
- **Type**: Ice spell (Ultimate)
- **Range**: Line attack

## Usage

Used by [[Mimi]] and elite ice mages of the [[Cult of Elements]] as a devastating ultimate attack that cuts through multiple enemies in a straight line.
