# Plot

The Cult of Gold is an ancient order of geologist-scientists, alchemists, and extreme material researchers who dedicate their lives to studying the deep, physical truths of the earth. To them, the divine is not found in the sky, but in the crystal lattices and molecular structures of the world. Believers in the God of Gold access the alteration magic field to physically manipulate materials, transmuting matter to understand its core properties.

## Background

The Cult arose in the harshest mountain ranges of the continent, born not out of desire for conquest, but an intense scientific curiosity about the bones of the earth.

### Early Life

Centuries ago, the first members were scattered miners and hermits. They discovered that by systematically studying the geologic resonance of a cavern, they could use alteration magic to shape the stone without tools. This led to the establishment of the first Great Academies.

### Turning Point 1

The "Discovery of the Core Element." The Cult's researchers successfully transmuted base granite into a highly conductive, near-indestructible metallic alloy. This technological leap formalized the Cult as the premier scientific institution for metallurgy and alchemy.

### Turning Point 2

The Great Scholar Schism. Half the Cult wanted to share their alteration discoveries with the [[Macht]] Empire to advance industrial civilization, while the purists insisted their magic must remain purely observational and academic to preserve the natural tectonic balance.

## Appearance

Their priests and scholars dress in heavy, soot-stained leather aprons over functional tunics, heavily laden with vials of acid, tuning forks, and geological hammers. They frequently wear thick protective goggles to shield their eyes from the blinding flash of transmutation magic.

## Personality

Deeply analytical, endlessly curious, and fiercely academic. A follower of Gold thinks in terms of half-lives, tensile strength, and atomic bonds. They are not interested in the heavens or ethereal spirits; they are grounded, literal, and treat magic as a rigorous science rather than a mystical religion.

# CHARACTER MAP

CHARACTER NAME: Cult of Gold (As a Faction Entity)

## ORIGIN OF URGE

In studying the catastrophic tectonic shifts of the ancient world, they learned that the only things that endure are the deep stones. They sought to understand the bedrock so they could never be crushed by it.

## CORE URGE

**To decode and master the physical properties of all matter.**
In order to survive / get my needs met, I must measure, test, and alter the fundamental materials of my environment.

## STATED BELIEF (LIE)

The God of Gold demands that we dissect the world to its purest atomic form, and only through perfect scientific understanding can we find salvation.

## THE CORE URGE AFFECTS ALL AREAS OF THEIR LIFE

### GOALS

To build an exhaustive encyclopedia of all material states, and to perfect the ultimate transmutation algorithm.

### RELATIONSHIPS

Collegial but emotionally distant. They view relationships much like chemical reactions: predictable, sometimes volatile, but ultimately just components interacting.

### LIFESTYLE

Sequestered in deep, heavily fortified mountain laboratories or vast, dusty libraries full of ore samples.

### PRESENTATION

Utilitarian and heavily equipped. They do not wear jewelry for aesthetics; any gold they wear is a calculated catalyst for their spells.

### DIALOGUE

"Faith will not stop the ceiling from collapsing, Priest. But knowing the sheer stress tolerance of basalt certainly will."
