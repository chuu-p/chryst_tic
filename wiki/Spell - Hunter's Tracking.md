# Spell: Hunter's Tracking

## Description

Hunter's Tracking is a passive ability that grants elite tracking abilities, allowing the user to follow targets across any terrain.

## Mechanics

- **Effect**: Elite tracking abilities
- **Type**: Passive
- **Range**: Any terrain

## Usage

Possessed by [[Tymo]] and trackers of the [[Cult of Gold]] who pursue targets across vast distances and difficult landscapes.
