# Spell: Holy Shield Parry

## Description

Holy Shield Parry is a holy spell that uses the holy shield to parry incoming blows, deflecting physical attacks with divine energy.

## Mechanics

- **Effect**: Parries incoming attacks using holy shield
- **Type**: Holy spell
- **Visual**: Golden shield deflection

## Usage

Used by [[Chryst]] and warriors of the [[Cult of Fayth]] to actively deflect blows in combat using the protective holy barrier.
