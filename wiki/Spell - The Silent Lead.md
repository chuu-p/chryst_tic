# Spell: The Silent Lead

## Description

The Silent Lead is an alteration spell that powers a suppressed rail gun sniper rifle using alteration magic, providing silent long-range firepower.

## Mechanics

- **Effect**: Powers suppressed rail gun sniper rifle
- **Type**: Alteration spell
- **Weapon**: Silent sniper rifle

## Usage

Used by [[Tymo]] and marksmen of the [[Cult of Gold]] for long-range assassination and precision strikes without revealing their position.
