# Scene: The First Mission

In the days following the raid, [[Chryst]] works tirelessly to prepare for the first official service of the [[Cult of Fayth]] in [[Bysfrost]]. He introduces the villagers to his unique form of magic: writing holy scripture on paper that, when prayed, manifests miracles. He sets up a central altar that broadcasts these prayers, healing the sick and purifying the village's water.

The villagers are skeptical at first, but they are won over by Chryst's sincerity and the effectiveness of his magic. [[Astryd]], however, remains baffled. In this new holy house, everyone is kind and protective of one another. "When the bandits return, you will all be killed, why aren't you training, making weapons and preparing for the attack?" she warns.

Chryst simply smiles. "Strength comes from the community, not from the individual. The goddess is with us, and we will be safe."

Astryd is still skeptical, but she finds herself respecting his leadership. His unwavering resolve reminds her of her own culture in [[Maxt]], though the philosophy is inverted; where Maxt demands individual strength for respect, Chryst earns it through collective faith.

On Sunday evening, as the first service concludes, the bandits return in force, seeking revenge. However, this time the village is prepared. Empowered by the collective fayth and the prayers broadcast from the altar, Chryst's power is amplified tenfold. He defeats the bandits without shedding a drop of blood, using his magic to ensnare them and force their surrender.

"Because the villagers have given me their fayth and the goddess has given me power," Chryst tells the defeated men, "I can resolve these conflicts without the need for deadly force." He explains that he is not there to unite the nordic tribes through blood, but to spread a message of peace and unity.

In a final act of grace, Chryst forgives the bandits. He allows them to live amongst the villagers on the condition that they repent and work for the good of the community.

Over the next two weeks, Chryst remains in Bysfrost to solidify the mission. He hires a local man to serve as the village priest, training him and Astryd in the ways of the [[Cult of Fayth]]. Under his guidance, the village begins to thrive.

However, the peace is short-lived. A messenger arrives from [[Bryskyr]] with urgent orders for [[Astryd]]. She is commanded to return to the city immediately—and she is ordered to arrest Chryst for the illegal use of magic without permission and bring him to [[Bryskyr]] for trial.

Astryd is torn between her duty to [[Maxt]] and her deep debt to the man who saved her. Hiding her true conflicting feelings, she tells Chryst she will escort him to [[Bryskyr]] under "guard" for his protection, though both know the true weight of the shackles that now hang over their journey.
As the sun sets, Chryst and Astryd leave Bysfrost behind, heading toward the next leg of their journey.

SUMMARY
[[Chryst]] establishes the [[Cult of Fayth]] in [[Bysfrost]], training the villagers and even forgiving the bandits. However, his illegal use of magic catches up with him when [[Astryd]] receives orders from [[Bryskyr]] to arrest him and bring him in for trial.
