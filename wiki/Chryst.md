## Background

In fantasy medieval Europe Chryst grew up as a normal [[Cult of Fayth|Priest]] mage apprentice with [[Chrysts Brother|his brother]], a hero apprentice in [[Yswyndt]] (modern day Iceland). After being invaded by the [[Maxt]] nation Chryst and his brother need to fight in the war. His brother gets fatally wounded and chryst performs a forbidden ritual, fuses their souls and freezes his heart.

After learning, that given enough fayth he can unfreeze and split souls, he makes it his mission to create a huge connected network of his religion and gain enough followers so he can generate enough fayth to unfreeze his brothers soul and revive him.

He departs from Iceland to missionize Europe, and arrives at [[Bysfrost]]. This is the story of his travels, missions and adventures that he and his crew experience on their way. During this campaign chryst helps more and more people but gets more and more obsessed with his task and looses balance in life. The main arc of him will be learning Temperance at the end of the story.
