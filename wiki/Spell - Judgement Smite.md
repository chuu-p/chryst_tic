# Spell: Judgement Smite

## Description

Judgement Smite is a holy spell that smites an enemy or sinner with massive holy damage, delivering divine punishment through concentrated Fayth energy.

## Mechanics

- **Effect**: Massive holy damage to enemies or sinners
- **Type**: Holy spell
- **Visual**: Blinding holy strike

## Usage

Used by [[Chryst]] and inquisitors of the [[Cult of Fayth]] to deliver divine justice against sinners and enemies of the faith.
