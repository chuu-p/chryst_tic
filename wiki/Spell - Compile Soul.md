# Spell: Compile Soul

## Description

Compile Soul is an unholy spell that merges multiple souls into a single vessel, creating a composite being with combined memories and traits.

## Mechanics

- **Effect**: Merges multiple souls into one vessel
- **Type**: Unholy spell
- **Result**: Creates a composite being

## Usage

Used by [[Chryst]] and practitioners of the [[Cult of Fayth]] to create powerful fused entities from multiple captured souls.
