# Themes

## The Seven Heavenly Virtues

The squad members are narratively tied to the seven heavenly virtues, contrasting against the deadly sins as part of their character arcs.

- **Temperance (vs Gluttony)**: **[[Chryst]]** - He is an obsessed workaholic who must learn work-life balance and not be consumed entirely by his mission.
- **Patience (vs Wrath)**: **[[Tymo]]** - Always in a good mood and never angry, he patiently waits for the precise moment to reclaim his homeland with minimal bloodshed.
- **Diligence (vs Sloth)**: **[[Styllfried]]** - Initially traumatized and refusing to fight ("sandbagging"), he must overcome his laziness to use his defensive void magic to protect others.
- **Chastity (vs Lust)**: **[[Berlynde]]** - She must learn to love herself from the inside and not rely on validation from others.
- **Kindness (vs Envy)**: **[[The Ronin]]** - Ironically a brutal soldier, he is too kind-hearted to want to kill his enemies. Chryst enables him to fight again by instantly healing his foes.
- **Humility (vs Pride)**: **[[Astryd]]** - A typical teenager who is extremely proud and thinks she knows everything, needing to learn true humility.
- **Charity (vs Greed)**: **[[Mimi]]** - Placing others' needs before her own. Currently a selfish, tsundere master mage, she must learn the ultimate value of selflessness.
