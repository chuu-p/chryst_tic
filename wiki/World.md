# Plot

I want to think of the world in this story as a kind of character, that changes over the course of the story.

The world is geographically shaped like the real world, and they have the technology of the 12th century, but magic exists and there are more people alive than in the real world back then.
The nordic countries take inspiration from Freljord in League of Legends, .

Regions:

- The 4 nordic countries
  - [[Yswyndt]] (Iceland)
  - [[Syrvann]] (Sweden)
  - [[Norholdt]] (Norway)
  - ? (Finland)
- The [[Macht]] Empire (Germany, have currently occupied much of the nordic countries, capital is Hamburg)
- The [[Demacia]] (France)
- the decentralized merchant state, which is a collection of some city states (Amsterdam, Cologne, etc.)
- The sand and desert people, with void magic like ikathia from league
- ... many more regions, like china, japan, russia, etc.

there is no chaos energy, or similar. there are just factions and humans and stuff that are acting selfishly in their best interest, like in our real world
there are people who pray to gods, which chrysts religion views as evil but they just have a different philosophy
like orcs who actually believe in the right of the strongest, so when you are stronger they obey you
