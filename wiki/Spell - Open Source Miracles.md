# Spell: Open Source Miracles

## Description

Open Source Miracles is a passive ability that leaves background scripts in allies' gear, providing automatic beneficial effects without active maintenance.

## Mechanics

- **Effect**: Background scripts in allies' gear for automatic effects
- **Type**: Passive
- **Duration**: Persistent until removed

## Usage

Possessed by [[Chryst]] and network priests of the [[Cult of Fayth]] who can bless equipment with self-running divine scripts.
