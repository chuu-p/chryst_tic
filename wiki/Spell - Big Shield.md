# Spell: Big Shield

## Description

Big Shield is a passive ability that blocks massive amounts of physical damage, providing superior protection in combat.

## Mechanics

- **Effect**: Blocks massive physical damage
- **Type**: Passive
- **Defense**: High physical damage reduction

## Usage

Possessed by [[Styllfried]] and void guardians who can shrug off devastating physical attacks that would kill lesser warriors.
