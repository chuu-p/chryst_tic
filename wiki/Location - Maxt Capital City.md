# Maxt Capital City / Hamburg

The belly of the beast, the sprawling metropolis and center of the Maxt Empire. Known as Hamburg in the modern context, this industrial city represents the pinnacle of Maxt power - a massive urban center of iron, steam, and surveillance that threatens to overwhelm all of Europe.

## Description

The Maxt Capital is a sprawling industrial metropolis characterized by iron frameworks, steam-powered machinery, and an omnipresent surveillance network. The city's sheer scale is overwhelming - a testament to Maxt engineering and authoritarian organization. It is a place where the air smells of coal smoke and the streets are patrolled by uniformed legions.

## Lore

The Maxt Capital represents the antithesis of everything the Cult of Fayth stands for - centralized control, authoritarian rule, and the belief that strength justifies any action. The empire's philosophy is that weaker nations must be conquered and united under a single iron banner "for their own good." Chryst realizes that spreading the Cult here will require not just faith, but strategy. The city's massive population makes it both a crucial target for the Fayth network and a dangerous den of shadow magic and deception.
