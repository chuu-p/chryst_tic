# Cult of Handrich

The predominant religion and socioeconomic philosophy of the opulent Byzantine merchant cities. They are the primary ideological antagonist of the story, deeply exploring the theme of "capitalism without temperance."

## Philosophy

The Cult of Handrich fundamentally believes that there are no truly evil people—only individuals operating in the unethical pursuit of pure profit optimization. To them, morality, empathy, and nature are merely market inefficiencies. They strip the land of raw soul-energy purely for profit, easily paying off corrupt governments to look the other way.

## Impact on the Story

They serve as a dark, structural mirror to Chryst's own obsessive efficiency. If Chryst chooses the failed path of his "Open Source Arc"—choosing to hoard his magical research, centralize his power, and enforce a rigid, proprietary monopoly on the Fayth network—he realizes he is functionally indistinguishable from the Cult of Handrich. He must witness their devastation to learn that power meant to save people can easily become power over people.
