# Spell: Insta Heal

## Description

Insta Heal is a holy spell that instantly heals a person from a distance without the need for physical contact, channeling Fayth across space.

## Mechanics

- **Effect**: Instant healing at range
- **Type**: Holy spell
- **Range**: Distance healing

## Usage

Used by [[Chryst]] and advanced healers of the [[Cult of Fayth]] to provide emergency healing without needing to reach the injured person.
