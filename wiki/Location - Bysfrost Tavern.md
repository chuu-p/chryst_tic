# Bysfrost Tavern

The central gathering place in Bysfrost village where Chryst first arrived after his sea journey from Yswyndt. This rowdy establishment is where Chryst first met Astryd and protected her with a Holy Shield, earning a death threat instead of gratitude.

## Description

A weathered timber building with frost-covered eaves and smoke rising from its chimney. Inside, the atmosphere is boisterous but weary, with rough-hewn tables, dim lighting, and the constant smell of ale and woodsmoke. The tavern serves as the social hub of the struggling village.

## Lore

The Bysfrost Tavern represents the tension between occupied peoples and their "protectors." When a villager challenged Astryd's claim that Maxt's presence was beneficial, the situation nearly boiled over. Chryst's instinctive use of Holy Shield to protect Astryd marked the beginning of their complicated relationship. His reward was Astryd's death threat: "Never cast a spell on me again, or I will kill you myself."
