# Brsykyr

The heavily surveilled, occupied capital of Norholdt - a brutalist city of cold stone, perpetual overcast skies, and deep paranoia. Brsykyr serves as the primary setting for Chryst's tournament arc, where he wins legal recognition for the Cult of Fayth through combat.

## Description

Brsykyr is a stark, brutalist city characterized by cold grey stone architecture, narrow streets, and an oppressive atmosphere of surveillance and fear. The city is under Maxt occupation, with a thriving underground culture of bloodsport and resistance. Overcast skies seem permanently fixed above its skyline.

## Lore

Brsykyr represents the clash of ideologies between the occupying Maxt Empire (strength is everything) and the growing Cult of Fayth (faith and unity). The tournament serves as a crucible where Chryst proves that true strength comes from collective belief rather than individual martial prowess. After Chryst's departure, Astryd remains as the founder of the community, though she eventually chooses to follow him.
