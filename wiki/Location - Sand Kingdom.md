# Sand Kingdom

Ygor's homeland, a vast desert kingdom ruled by a king who forbids free travel. The Sand Kingdom is home to brutal samurai warriors and void magic practitioners, with a culture that contrasts sharply with the icy Nordic regions of the story.

## Description

The Sand Kingdom is an expanse of golden dunes, scorching sun, and ancient desert cities built from sandstone and marble. The kingdom is ruled by a monarch who keeps his princes confined to the capital, forbidding them from traveling freely. The culture values martial prowess, with samurai warriors wielding twin katanas and practicing void magic.

## Lore

The Sand Kingdom represents the "warm" counterpart to the icy North - a place of fire, sand, and martial honor. Ygor, too kind to kill his enemies, struggles with the kingdom's brutal expectations. His virtue of Kindness (vs Envy) clashes with the warrior culture. When he joins Chryst's party, his "wie jeder andre mann" personality and melancholic battlefield singing provide a unique contrast to the Nordic stoicism of the rest of the group. The synergy with Chryst allows Ygor to fight without holding back, knowing Chryst will heal his enemies.
