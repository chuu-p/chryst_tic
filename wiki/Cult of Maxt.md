# Cult of Maxt

The Cult of Maxt stems from the sweeping, militaristic hegemony of the [[Macht]] Empire (often stylized as MaxT). Their ideology is built around supremacy, deception, and the absolute victory of the state. They are currently occupying vast territories of the Nordic states.

## Background

Rooted in the historical frailty and chaos of fragmented nations, the Empire learned that survival belongs only to those who can script the reality of their enemies before the battle even begins.

### Early Life

The Cult evolved from ancient spymasters and imperial strategists who realized that warfare is too chaotic if fought only with steel. They began exploring the edges of the shadow magic field.

### Turning Point 1

The militarization of the Cult. Shadow magic was successfully integrated into the military academies, creating a terrifying breed of soldier-mages who could shatter morale before firing a single shot.

### Turning Point 2

The Northern Expansion. The Cult was unleashed en masse against the Nordic nations, turning the invasion of Norholdt into a near-flawless psychological obliteration of the defending armies.

## Philosophy and Magic

The God of Maxt teaches that life is entirely a game of shadows and theater—a grand play where the only sin is losing. To them, the "truth" is merely whatever narrative the victor decides to write. Believers in Maxt are Shadow Mages. They specialize in mental manipulation, illusions, and lethal tactile spells—such as the dreaded touch of death.

For a follower of Maxt, deceiving an opponent is considered the highest form of prayer. Their shadow magic functions by obscuring the sensory inputs of their enemies, rewriting their perceptions, or striking directly at their mental resilience, allowing them to conquer with minimal physical resistance.

## Appearance and Priests

### Appearance

Their priests are akin to officers of a dark military intelligence. They wear severe, high-collared uniforms in blacks and crimson, adorned with sharp geometric insignia. Their faces are often partially concealed behind visors or masks to protect their own minds while projecting authority and terror.

### Personality

Cold, manipulative, and fiercely disciplined. They possess a grim charm—the sort of polite sociopathy that can order a city's destruction with a gentle smile. They appreciate the aesthetics of dominance and despise vulnerability.

## ORIGIN OF URGE

Faced with the historical frailty and chaos of fragmented nations, the Empire learned that survival belongs only to those who can script the reality of their enemies before the battle even begins.

## CORE URGE

**To rewrite the world into an ordered empire where we hold all the cards.**
In order to survive and get my needs met, I must deceive, dominate, and dismantle the mental defenses of my opposition.

## STATED BELIEF (LIE)

We are bringing order and enlightenment to a messy, chaotic world. Pain is merely the price of civilization.

## THE CORE URGE AFFECTS ALL AREAS OF THEIR LIFE

### GOALS

The total conquest of the continent, starting with the suppression of the Nordic states.

### RELATIONSHIPS

Built on paranoia, hierarchy, and leverage. Trust is an illusion; only utility matters.

### LIFESTYLE

Strictly regimented, heavily surveilled, and fiercely competitive.

### PRESENTATION

Imposing, immaculate, and utterly devoid of warmth.

### DIALOGUE

"You fight for a god who demands you love your enemy. My god demands only that I win. Guess who has the advantage?"
