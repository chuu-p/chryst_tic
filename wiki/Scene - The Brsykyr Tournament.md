The Brsykyr Arena was a hollow in the earth where the wind whistled like a dying man’s breath. The soil was packed tight with the blood of centuries. [[Chryst]] stood in the center, his robes fluttering, his staff held loosely in his hand. The crowd roared—a sound of hunger, not admiration.

**Round 1: The Hollow Man**
His first opponent was a man whose eyes were lost to his own darkness. A prisoner who fought only for the chance to breathe free air again. His strikes were heavy with desperation but lacked the precision of a clear mind. Chryst moved like water, yielding to the force and redirecting it. With a sharp tap of his staff against the man’s temple, the fight was over.
"You fight the world because you cannot fight yourself," Chryst murmured as the man fell. "The cage you seek to escape is within."

**Round 2: The Honest Blade**
The second was a warrior of honor, a veteran whose steel moved with the grace of experience. It was a dance of respect. The warrior fought well, his blade missing Chryst’s throat by a hair’s breadth. But in the end, Chryst’s magic—a subtle manipulation of the air—threw the man off balance. The warrior looked at Chryst, saw the tip of the staff at his chest, and bowed.
"You have the strength of a mountain and the stillness of a lake," the warrior said. "I yield to a better man."

**Round 3: The Greedy Shadow**
The final match of the day was against a man who smelled of poison and ambition. He fought dirty, throwing ash into Chryst's eyes and feigning injury to draw him in. He wanted the prize at any cost, his soul black with the rot of greed.
"Balance," Chryst whispered, blinking away the stinging dust. "You seek to tip the scales too far."
The opponent lunged with a hidden dagger, but [[Goddess]]’s light flared from Chryst’s chest. The unholy malice in the man’s heart acted as a lightning rod for the holy magic. The blast threw him back, his own treachery turned against him.
"To win is good," Chryst told the fallen, dying man. "To win at the cost of your soul is to lose everything. You chose the dark, and the light has judged you."

SUMMARY
[[Chryst]] participates in the first day of the Brsykyr tournament. He defeats three opponents: a desperate prisoner, a noble warrior, and a treacherous cheat. Each fight serves as a lesson in faith, balance, and the consequences of sin.
