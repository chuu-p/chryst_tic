# Spell: Recall to Blade

## Description

Recall to Blade is a samurai spell that sets a blade in the ground to later recall to it, allowing instant teleportation to the blade's location.

## Mechanics

- **Effect**: Sets blade in ground, later recalls to it
- **Type**: Samurai spell
- **Movement**: Instant recall

## Usage

Used by [[Ygor]] and samurai of the [[Cult of Fayth]] to create teleportation anchors for tactical repositioning in combat.
