# Spell: Okkam's Mindrazor

## Description

Okkam's Mindrazor is a shadow spell that summons phantasmal weapons which shred consciousness and reason, destroying an enemy's ability to think.

## Mechanics

- **Effect**: Phantasmal weapons that shred consciousness and reason
- **Type**: Shadow spell
- **Target**: Mental faculties

## Usage

Used by [[Berlynde]] and shadow mages of the [[Cult of Maxt]] to incapacitate enemies by destroying their rational thought processes.
