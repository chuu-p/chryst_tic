# Cult of Maxt Military Bases

The network of military installations and shadow magic academies that support the Maxt Empire's occupation of Nordic territories. These bases serve as centers of industrial weaponry manufacturing, shadow magic training, and authoritarian control.

## Description

Brutalist fortresses of iron and grey stone, surrounded by high walls, watchtowers, and training grounds. The air is thick with coal smoke and the acrid scent of shadow magic. Inside, the atmosphere is one of rigid discipline, surveillance, and the constant drilling of soldiers. The bases house both conventional military units and shadow mages who practice sensory manipulation.

- **Surveillance Centers** - Monitoring occupied territories

## Lore

The Cult of Maxt utilizes shadow magic - their god promotes deceit and victory at any cost. Their spells manipulate the sensory inputs and mental fortitude of enemies, casting powerful illusions. These military bases represent the industrial, authoritarian might that threatens the Nordic way of life. The bases' philosophy that "strength is everything" directly opposes the Cult of Fayth's radical compassion. Astryd's transformation from a Maxt knight to a follower of Fayth represents the rejection of everything these bases stand for.
