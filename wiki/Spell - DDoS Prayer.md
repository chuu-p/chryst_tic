# Spell: DDoS Prayer

## Description

DDoS Prayer is a holy spell that routes aggressive sensory overload inputs into an enemy's mind, overwhelming their consciousness through prayer bombardment.

## Mechanics

- **Effect**: Sensory overload attack on enemy mind
- **Type**: Holy spell
- **Method**: Prayer bombardment

## Usage

Used by [[Chryst]] and network warriors of the [[Cult of Fayth]] to disable enemies by flooding their mental processes with divine noise.
