# Ancient Stone Fountain in Brsykyr

A secluded stone fountain in Brsykyr where the moon reflects like a silver eye in the still waters. This is the intimate site where Chryst baptized Axtritt, marking her rebirth as Astryd and her conversion to the Cult of Fayth.

## Description

An ancient stone fountain with weathered carvings and still, dark waters that reflect the moonlight. The cobbles around it are wet with spray, and the fountain has a timeless quality - a rare beautiful spot in the brutalist city. At night, the fountain becomes ethereal, with moonlight clinging to the water like a shroud.

## Lore

This fountain is one of the most spiritually significant locations in Brsykyr. In the cold of night, Chryst guided Axtritt into the water, telling her "You are reborn. You are Astryd. You are the shield of the Fayth." The baptism marked Astryd's complete transformation from a Maxt weapon to a woman of faith. For the first time in years, the hollow ache in her chest was gone. This location symbolizes the power of the Cult of Fayth to offer true rebirth, not just forgiveness.
