# Frozen Hell Dungeon

A forgotten dungeon on a remote northern island, a deserted mine carved into the permafrost where time itself seems to stand still. The deepest level is the coldest place in the known world - a natural freezer where souls can be stored indefinitely to serve as the primary battery for the entire Fayth network.

## Description

The dungeon descends through layers of spiritual corruption, temptation, and despair, each floor testing a different aspect of the visitor's character. The air grows progressively colder with each level, until at the bottom, the temperature is so low that souls can be frozen without degradation. The mine was designed as a physical manifestation of the world's underworld.

## Lore

Originally built by Tymo's halfling ancestors as a safeguard, the dungeon was meant to test whether humanity is worthy of controlling death itself. Chryst repurposed it as the primary infrastructure for his Fayth network - a massive Frozen Heart battery that could buffer surges during the Sunday Pulse. The dungeon sealed behind them after the ritual, its original purpose fulfilled. Each party member faced their deepest failure here, making it as much a spiritual trial as a technical solution.
