# Maxt Empire

The industrial, authoritarian empire that occupies much of the Nordic countries and threatens all of Europe. Centered in the Maxt Capital City (Hamburg), the empire represents the antithesis of the Cult of Fayth - a regime built on shadow magic, deceit, and the philosophy that strength justifies any action.

## Description

The Maxt Empire is a vast industrial powerhouse characterized by brutalism, steam-powered machinery, iron frameworks, and an omnipresent surveillance network. The empire's military is highly organized, equipped with industrial weaponry and shadow magic. Their philosophy is pragmatic and ruthless - they believe weaker nations must be conquered and united under a single iron banner "for their own good."

## Lore

The Maxt Empire serves as the primary opposing force to Chryst's mission. Their god promotes deceit and victory at any cost, with spells that manipulate sensory inputs and mental fortitude. The empire's occupation of Nordic lands creates the central conflict of the story. However, the empire isn't cartoonishly evil - they genuinely believe they're uniting fractured nations for their own good. This philosophical clash between Maxt's "strength is everything" and Fayth's "radical compassion" drives much of the story's tension.
