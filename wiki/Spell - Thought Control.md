# Spell: Thought Control

## Description

Thought Control is a shadow spell that creates powerful illusions and hallucinations to control minds. It also passively makes the user invisible to normal detection.

## Mechanics

- **Effect**: Illusions and hallucinations that control minds; passive invisibility
- **Type**: Shadow spell
- **Special**: Passive invisibility while active

## Usage

Used by [[Berlynde]] and mind mages of the [[Cult of Maxt]] to manipulate enemies and remain unseen while exerting mental dominance.
