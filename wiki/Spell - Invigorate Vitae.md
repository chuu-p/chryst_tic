# Spell: Invigorate Vitae

## Description

Invigorate Vitae is an unholy spell that heals the caster's wounds by drinking the blood of a freshly killed corpse, converting vitality to life force.

## Mechanics

- **Effect**: Heals wounds by consuming corpse blood
- **Type**: Unholy spell
- **Method**: Blood drinking from fresh corpses

## Usage

Used by [[Chryst]] and dark practitioners of the [[Cult of Fayth]] who embrace blood magic to rapidly heal in the field.
