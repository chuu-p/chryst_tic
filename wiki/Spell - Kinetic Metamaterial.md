# Spell: Kinetic Metamaterial

## Description

Kinetic Metamaterial is a passive ability where altered clothing acts as a non-Newtonian fluid, hardening on impact to absorb blows.

## Mechanics

- **Effect**: Clothing hardens on impact like non-Newtonian fluid
- **Type**: Passive
- **Protection**: Impact absorption

## Usage

Possessed by [[Tymo]] and enhanced warriors of the [[Cult of Gold]] who wear specially altered garments that provide dynamic protection.
