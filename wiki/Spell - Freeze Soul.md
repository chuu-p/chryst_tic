# Spell: Freeze Soul

## Description

Freeze Soul is an unholy spell that freezes a soul in icy resin, preventing it from departing to the Aether after death.

## Mechanics

- **Effect**: Traps a soul in physical form using cyan resin
- **Type**: Unholy spell
- **Usage**: Prevents soul departure to the Aether

## Usage

Used by [[Chryst]] and priests of the [[Cult of Fayth]] to intercept souls before they can pass on, enabling further unholy rituals or soul compilation.
