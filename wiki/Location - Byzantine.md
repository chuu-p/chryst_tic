# Byzantine

The opulent, wealthy merchant city-state and capitalist mecca of the world. Known for its extreme wealth, corruption, and decentralized collection of city-states (Amsterdam, Cologne, etc.), Byzantine represents the pinnacle of commercial power and moral decay through unchecked capitalism.

## Description

Byzantine is a glittering metropolis of merchant palaces, golden spires, and bustling marketplaces. The city exudes wealth and excess, where trade routes converge and gold flows like water. However, beneath the opulence lies deep corruption, with merchant guilds controlling every aspect of life and the poor struggling in the shadows of grand architecture.

## Lore

Byzantine serves as the counterpoint to Chryst's obsessive mission. When the dangerously obsessed Chryst finally tracks down Frydemann expecting a serious deity of magic, he finds a cynical, retired hedonist. Frydemann points out an old woman carrying a basket of apples, teaching Chryst that "Its ordinary to love the beautiful, its beautiful to love the ordinary." This becomes a vital counter-weight to Chryst's "Lonely God" descent, teaching him to accept mundane life.
