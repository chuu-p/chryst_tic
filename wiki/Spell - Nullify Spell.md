# Spell: Nullify Spell

## Description

Nullify Spell is a void spell that nullifies spells cast near the user, creating a zone where magic cannot take effect.

## Mechanics

- **Effect**: Nullifies nearby spells
- **Type**: Void spell
- **Area**: Around user

## Usage

Used by [[Styllfried]] and void mages to disrupt enemy spellcasters and create magic-free zones in combat.
