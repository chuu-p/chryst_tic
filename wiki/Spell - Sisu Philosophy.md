# Spell: Sisu Philosophy

## Description

Sisu Philosophy is a passive ability that grants radical inner freedom, preventing fear or pain-shock from affecting the user's mental state.

## Mechanics

- **Effect**: Prevents fear and pain-shock
- **Type**: Passive
- **Mental**: Radical inner freedom

## Usage

Possessed by [[Tymo]] and stoic warriors of the [[Cult of Gold]] who refuse to be mentally broken by trauma or terror.
