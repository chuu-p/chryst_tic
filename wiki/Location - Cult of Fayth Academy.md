# Cult of Fayth Headquarters / Academy

The central academy and headquarters of the Cult of Fayth, established by Chryst during his "Open Source Arc." This is where he decentralizes his power, teaching others to use holy programming and open-sourcing his miracles for anyone to use.

## Description

A brutalist stone complex - true to Chryst's architectural philosophy - with functional, unadorned buildings arranged around a central church. The academy features scriptoriums for writing holy code, classrooms for teaching Fayth programming, and a massive central church that serves as the primary node in the Fayth network. The buildings are deeply practical, rejecting unnecessary ornamentation.

## Important Features

- **Scriptorium** - Where holy code and prayer-scripts are written
- **Classrooms** - Teaching holy programming to new priests
- **Central Church** - The primary network node with massive Frozen Heart storage
- **Holy Language labs** - Where students learn to write conditional syntax and arrays
- **Sunday Pulse chamber** - Where the weekly synchronized prayer occurs

## Lore

The academy represents Chryst's transformation from a proprietary, isolated dictator of magic to a radical open-source evangelist. Instead of accepting the Government Mandate (authoritarian control), Chryst chooses to teach others his miracles. The academy becomes the intellectual heart of the Cult, training new priests in holy programming and network maintenance. It's here that Zael learns temperance and compassion, while teaching Chryst to let go of absolute control.
