# Scene: Arrival at Beisfrost Tavern

[[Chryst]] arrives in [[Beisfrost]] after a few weeks at sea, traveling from his homeland of [[Yswyndt]]. The village is cold and weary, its atmosphere heavy with the weight of the ongoing war and the "protection" of the [[Maxt]] nation.

He heads to the [[Beisfrost Tavern]] to seek warmth and a place to rest. Inside, he finds a rowdy scene dominated by [[Astryd]], a knight of Maxt, who is drinking heavily and boasting to the locals. She insists that they should be grateful to Maxt for sending her to protect them from bandits. A villager challenges her, pointing out that Maxt's presence feels more like an occupation meant to scout defenses for a forced annexation.

The tension nearly boils over when a villager tries to punch Astryd. Without thinking, Chryst uses his faith to manifest a "bubble" around her, deflecting the blow effortlessly. The tavern falls into a stunned silence as Chryst, weary from his travels, retires to his room for the night.
NOTE: bubble is the commoners name for a spell: Holy Shield is a [[Cult of Fayth]] spell that prevents all damage to the target for a short period of time (update this here and add create a spell entry based on T Spell.md)
NOTE: Astryd confronts him directly after bubbling her. Who does he think he is? Does he think she needs protection? She is by far the strongest here. She tells him to never cast a spell on her again or she will kill him. He says he is just a priest and wants no trouble. This will come up later, when he saves her later.  

SUMMARY
[[Chryst]] arrives in [[Beisfrost]] and visits the local tavern. There, he overhears [[Astryd]] boasting about [[Maxt]]'s protection and intervenes in a physical altercation using a protective bubble.
