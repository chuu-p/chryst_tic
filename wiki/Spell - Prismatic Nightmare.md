# Spell: Prismatic Nightmare

## Description

Prismatic Nightmare is a combo spell requiring both [[Mimi]] and [[Berlynde]]. Berlynde creates a shadow illusion while Mimi fires a prism cannon into it, creating blinding clones with burn damage.

## Mechanics

- **Effect**: Shadow illusion + prism cannon = blinding clones with burn damage
- **Type**: Combo spell
- **Requires**: Mimi (Cult of Elements) + Berlynde (Cult of Maxt)

## Usage

A cooperative spell cast by [[Mimi]] and [[Berlynde]] together, combining ice/light magic with shadow illusions to create a devastating area effect.
