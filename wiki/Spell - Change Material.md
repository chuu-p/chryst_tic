# Spell: Change Material

## Description

Change Material is an alteration spell that changes any material into another material, allowing transmutation of physical substances.

## Mechanics

- **Effect**: Transmutes any material into another
- **Type**: Alteration spell
- **Range**: Any material

## Usage

Used by [[Tymo]] and alchemists of the [[Cult of Gold]] to transform resources and create valuable materials from common substances.
