# Zael

A brilliant, hyper-competent teenage rebel and combat prodigy from the Noxian territories. He is unapologetically cool, plays by his own rules, and excels in high-speed assassination magic.

## Background

Zael grew up in the oppressive, highly structured, and violently practical military regime of Noxus. He despised the rigid rules and learned to survive by exploiting the system, eventually defecting to become a masterful, rule-breaking rebel. When Chryst decides to follow his "Open Source Arc" and establish a magical academy to distribute his miracles, Zael joins as his most talented, albeit insubordinate, student.

## Appearance

Edgy, agile, deeply scarred for his young age, often seen casually tossing a dagger or perched in high places. He deliberately embraces a gritty, punk-inspired aesthetic that radically clashes with the heavy, disciplined armor of the rest of the setting.

## Personality

Arrogant, fiercely independent, but highly capable. He hides a deep desire for a reliable mentor figure behind an impenetrable shell of anti-authoritarian coolness.

### GOALS

To prove to the world that he does not need anyone's help or charity, while secretly yearning for structure and a found family that won't exploit or betray him.

### RELATIONSHIPS

- **Chryst**: They share a reluctant but profound master/apprentice dynamic.
  - **What Chryst teaches Zael**: Temperance, true compassion, and the painful lesson that absolute freedom without structure often leads to collateral damage and chaos.
  - **What Zael teaches Chryst**: How to let go of absolute, paranoid control. Zael forces Chryst to break his own rigid rules, showing him that he cannot—and should not—shoulder the burden of godhood completely alone.

### LIFESTYLE

A vagabond thrill-seeker, taking incredibly high-risk contracts just for the adrenaline and to prove his independence.

### PRESENTATION

A snarky, excessively cool prodigy who refuses to take the impending doom of the world seriously.

### DIALOGUE

"Rules are just panicked suggestions written by dead guys with no imagination. Relax, preachy. Watch this."
