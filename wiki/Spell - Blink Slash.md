# Spell: Blink Slash

## Description

Blink Slash is a samurai spell that dashes in a line dealing damage to all enemies along the path, combining teleportation with blade work.

## Mechanics

- **Effect**: Dashes in a line, damages all enemies
- **Type**: Samurai spell
- **Movement**: Blink dash

## Usage

Used by [[Ygor]] and samurai of the [[Cult of Fayth]] to quickly close distance and strike multiple enemies in a single motion.
