# Spell: Judging Slash

## Description

Judging Slash is a fayth spell that slashes an enemy and only cuts them if they are deemed a sinner by divine judgment.

## Mechanics

- **Effect**: Slashes enemy, only cuts if deemed sinner
- **Type**: Fayth spell
- **Judgment**: Divine assessment

## Usage

Used by [[Ygor]] and samurai inquisitors of the [[Cult of Fayth]] to strike only those who deserve punishment according to divine law.
