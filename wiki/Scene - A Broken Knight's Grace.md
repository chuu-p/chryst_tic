# Scene: A Broken Knight's Grace

The sun rises over a scarred [[Bysfrost]]. While the villagers are safe, the atmosphere is far from celebratory. The [[Bysfrost Mayor]] and the local townsfolk gather around the injured [[Astryd]], but instead of offering thanks, they berate her for her incompetence. They mock the "great nation of [[Maxt]]" for sending a knight who couldn't even handle a band of bandits without the help of a wandering stranger.

Astryd is mentally broken. In her culture, strength and martial prowess are everything. To be saved by a man from [[Yswyndt]]—the very land her people are currently conquering—is a source of deep shame. She feels she has failed her nation and her identity.

[[Chryst]] intervenes once more, but this time with words instead of shields. He speaks of redemption and the fact that everyone can be led astray. He tells her that while she failed in her duty of force, she has been given a chance to find a new path through good deeds.

Astryd looks up at him, her eyes red with frustration and shame. She hates him in this moment—not just for saving her life when she felt she was failing her nation, but for stripping away the "heroic" death she craves. To her prideful [[Maxt]] culture, a death in battle is far more glorious than the humiliation of being rescued by a wandering priest from [[Yswyndt]]. "You saved my life," she whispers, "but do you now also want to save my dignity?"

Chryst shakes his head slowly. "No," he replies. "I do not seek to save your pride. I simply follow the way my goddess leads me."

The Astryd suddenly realizes, that chryst has demonstrated, that his religion is actually stronger than the [[Cult of Maxt]], and the bandits too, and she is alive because of the [[Cult of Fayth]], so she starts to believe in the Cult of fayth and becomes chrysts first follower of his new religion and develops a crush on him. Her old world is gone, and the man who saved her offers no easy comfort for her ego. Her path forward will be to help chryst from behind the scenes in maxt by manipulating key people to help chryst. She will regain faith not in her nation or her title, but in herself—developing an individual sense of worth and morals that is not tied to being a knight of Maxt.

Seeing the village's need for hope and direction, Chryst asks the Mayor for permission to establish a community of the [[Cult of Fayth]]. Moved by Chryst's actions during the raid, the Mayor agrees, giving Chryst the space to build his first church.

SUMMARY
The villagers mock [[Astryd]] for her failure, leaving her humiliated. [[Chryst]] comforts her with a message of redemption and gains permission from the [[Bysfrost Mayor]] to establish a church.
