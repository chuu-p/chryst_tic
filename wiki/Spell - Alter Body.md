# Spell: Alter Body

## Description

Alter Body is an alteration spell that alters the caster's body to be stronger, faster, and more durable, enhancing physical capabilities.

## Mechanics

- **Effect**: Enhances strength, speed, and durability
- **Type**: Alteration spell
- **Target**: Self

## Usage

Used by [[Tymo]] and physical enhancement mages of the [[Cult of Gold]] to temporarily boost their physical combat abilities.
