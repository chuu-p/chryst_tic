# Spell: Silence the Room

## Description

Silence the Room is a void spell that slams the shield creating a dome of void that cancels all active spells and prevents further casting within the area.

## Mechanics

- **Effect**: Dome of void cancels active spells and prevents casting
- **Type**: Void spell
- **Area**: Dome

## Usage

Used by [[Styllfried]] and void masters to completely shut down magical combat in an area, leaving enemies defenseless.
