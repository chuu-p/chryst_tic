# Scene: The First Mission

In the days following the raid, [[Chryst]] works tirelessly to prepare for the first official service of the [[Cult of Fayth]] in [[Beisfrost]]. He introduces the villagers to his unique form of magic: writing holy scripture on paper that, when prayed, manifests miracles. He sets up a central altar that broadcasts these prayers, healing the sick and purifying the village's water.

NOTE: The villagers are skeptical at first, but they are won over by Chryst's sincerity and the effectiveness of his magic. Astryd is baffled, because in this new holy house everyone is nice to each other and worries, that when the bandits return, they will be killed. Chryst assures her, that strength comes from the community, not from the individual. He also tells her, that the goddess is with them, and that they will be safe. Astryd is still skeptical, but she is willing to trust him, because she sees him as a strong leader, but with a different philosophy, which reminds her of her own culture a bit. The difference is, that back in [[Maxt]], everyone has to be strong to be respected.

On Sunday evening, as the first service concludes, the bandits return in force, seeking revenge. However, this time the village is prepared. Empowered by the collective fayth and the prayers broadcast from the altar, Chryst's power is amplified tenfold. He defeats the bandits without shedding a drop of blood, using his magic to ensnare them and force their surrender.

NOTE: Chryst tells them, that because the goddess has given him power, he can resolve these conflicts and fights without having to kill anyone. He will be not be the one to unite the nordic tribes, but will spread his message of peace and unity, and that the people should spread his message far and wide through the land. 
NOTE: Chryst even forgives the bandits and allows them to live amongst the other villagers, because they have repented and will work for the good of the community.
NOTE: Chryst hires a local man to serve as the village priest, and training him and Astryd in the ways of the Cult of Fayth for 2 weeks.

NOTE: after 2 weeks, the village is thriving, and the cult of fayth is well-established. A messenger arrives from [[Baergen]] with orders for [[Astryd]] to return to the city and to arrest Chryst for using magic without permission and take him to Baergen, too.

Following the victory, [[Astryd]] receives orders to return to [[Bergen]]. Hiding her true feelings, she hires Chryst to escort her, claiming she needs a guard for her protection—though both know he is the one who truly saved her. 

As the sun sets, Chryst and Astryd leave Beisfrost behind, heading toward the next leg of their journey.

SUMMARY
[[Chryst]] establishes the [[Cult of Fayth]] mission in [[Beisfrost]], defeats a second bandit attack through non-violent faith-based magic, and departs for [[Bergen]] with [[Astryd]].
