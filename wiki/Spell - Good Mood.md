# Spell: Good Mood

## Description

Good Mood is a passive ability that allows the user to choose joy and patience regardless of external horrors or stressful situations.

## Mechanics

- **Effect**: Chooses joy and patience regardless of external conditions
- **Type**: Passive
- **Mental**: Emotional regulation

## Usage

Possessed by [[Tymo]] and disciplined members of the [[Cult of Gold]] who maintain psychological stability in the face of nightmare scenarios.
