# Spell: Mystifying Miasma

## Description

Mystifying Miasma is a shadow spell that creates a numbing fog causing listlessness and stumbling in all who breathe it.

## Mechanics

- **Effect**: Numbing fog causing listlessness and stumbling
- **Type**: Shadow spell
- **Area**: Fog cloud

## Usage

Used by [[Berlynde]] and shadow mages of the [[Cult of Maxt]] to slow enemy movement and reduce their combat effectiveness.
