# Ygor

A brutal prince swordfighter from the Sand Kingdom who wields two samurai swords, serving as the final main addition to the squad.

## Themes
- **Archetype**: The "wie jeder andre mann" character, and a melancholic warrior who is too kind to kill.
- **Virtue**: **Kindness (vs Envy)**. He is simply too nice to kill his enemies and sings "I fall in love too easily" melancholically on the battlefield. 
- **OST**: [Wie jeder andre Mann](https://youtu.be/UY3RF8FpCoM)

## Background and Relationships
- **The King**: His father forbids him to travel freely and be with his love, wanting to keep him in the capital.
- **Styllfried**: As they are both mature "adults" in the group, Ygor naturally becomes Styllfried's closest friend.
- **Synergy with Chryst**: When Chryst is in his party, Chryst can instantly heal anyone close to death. This allows Ygor to fight without holding back, knowing Chryst will save the enemies from dying and thus freeing him from his guilty conscience.

## Abilities & Combat
- He is a Samurai magic sword fighter who is **physically gifted**.
- **Samurai Magic**
  - `[[dual wield]]` (passive) - Uses two katanas to fight enemies.
  - `[[blink slash]]` (active) - Dashes in a line, dealing damage to all enemies in his path. A hard engage.
  - `[[recall to blade]]` (active) - Sets his blade in the ground to which he can later recall. An effective disengage.
- **Fayth Magic Empowered Swords**
  - `[[judging slash]]` (active) - Slashes an enemy and cuts them only if the sword deems them a sinner. It harmlessly bounces off if they have a pure heart.
