# Yswyndt Southern Gates / The Trenches

The battlefield where the Maxt Empire invaded Yswyndt, and the site where Helfrych performed the forbidden soul-fusion ritual to become Chryst. Known as The Trenches, this location is where the peaceful village's innocence died and the legend of Chryst began.

## Description

A frozen wasteland of blood-stained snow, trench networks, and the debris of war. The Southern Gates mark the entrance to Yswyndt valley, where the Maxt vanguard first breached the defenses. The Trenches are filled with the ghosts of the fallen, the ground frozen solid with crimson ice, and the air permanently cold from the residual magic of the soul-fusion ritual.

## Lore

This is the most significant battlefield in Chryst's early life. When Carolyn sacrificed herself to take Berlynde's Touch of Death, Helfrych refused to accept her death. Performing the forbidden ritual of soul-fusion, heart-freezing, and flesh-communion, he merged with Carolyn's essence to become Chryst - a twin-hearted messiah born of blood and cold cyan resin. The trench remains frozen year-round, a monument to the birth of the Cult of Fayth's founder.
