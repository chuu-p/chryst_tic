# Yswyndt Monastery

The monastery where Chryst (then Helfrych) trained as a priest under the tutelage of Fyrchtegott. This is where the foundations of the Cult of Fayth were laid through the study of unholy arts and the translation of the Bible into holy programming code.

## Description

A weathered stone monastery huddled against the harsh Nordic winds, with ice-clad walls and narrow windows that let in minimal light. Inside, the atmosphere is one of cold pragmatism and scholarly devotion, where ancient texts are studied and forbidden rituals are practiced behind altar-shrouds.

## Lore

Under Fyrchtegott's chilling pragmatism, Chryst mastered the Holy Language to translate the Bible - a massive program written by Frydemann. The monastery represents the bridge between primitive pagan magic and structured, computational Fayth magic. It was here that Chryst learned prayers are literal source code, and that magic is a tool of order rather than just prayer. The monastery's cold, bureaucratic approach to death directly shaped Chryst's later utilitarian worldview.
